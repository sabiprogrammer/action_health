About AHII

Action Health International Institute is a leading institution dedicated to advancing public health and promoting health equity across Africa and globally.

Who We Are

Action Health International Institute is a leading institution dedicated to advancing public health and promoting health equity across Africa and globally. With a commitment to empowering professionals in the health sector, the institute fosters research, education, and advocacy to address pressing health challenges. We focus on providing public health professionals with the necessary skills, resources, and opportunities to implement impactful, sustainable health solutions within their communities.

Vision

To be a globally recognized leader in advancing health equity by equipping public health professionals with the knowledge, skills, and resources to drive positive health outcomes and foster social justice in health.

Mission

Our mission is to promote health equity across diverse populations by providing high-quality education, research, and advocacy opportunities for public health professionals. We aim to build a network of experts capable of addressing global health challenges, with a particular focus on underserved communities in Africa.

What Makes Us Different

We are dedicated to advancing public health knowledge and fostering professional development. Explore our extensive collection of peer-reviewed journals, covering a wide range of topics.

Objectives

  • To empower public health professionals with the tools and knowledge to address health disparities.
  • To promote evidence-based health policies and practices that improve health outcomes.
  • To foster cross-disciplinary collaborations that tackle complex health issues at local, regional, and global levels.
  • To contribute to the development of sustainable health systems that prioritize equity, access, and inclusion.

Core Values

  • Equity: Commitment to ensuring that everyone has access to quality healthcare, regardless of their socio-economic background.
  • Integrity: Upholding the highest standards of ethical conduct in all activities and partnerships.
  • Collaboration: Encouraging partnerships and teamwork to achieve collective health goals.
  • Innovation: Continuously seeking new and creative solutions to health challenges.
  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards of quality in education, research, and health practice.
  • Sustainability: Promoting practices that ensure long-term positive health outcomes.

Core Areas of Interest

  1. Epidemiology and Disease Prevention
  2. Health Promotion and Education
  3. Environmental Health and Sustainability
  4. Healthcare Systems and Policy
  5. Global Health Challenges and Interventions

AHII Membership

Why Become a Member of AHII?

  • AHII is a global public health institute of professionals in public health with the aim of making a lasting impression to improve society. We work very hard to improve the lives of people all around the world.
  • We are an organization limited by guarantee and independent of both governmental and political control. AHII is sustained through membership subscription and donations from our valued members across the globe and well-wishers.
  • In addition, membership of AHII gives you exclusive access to our peer-reviewed journals, including the Action Health International Journal, and participation in our conferences globally.

Membership Levels

  1. Fellow (FAHII)
  2. Executive (EAHII)
  3. Associate (AAHII)
  4. Student (SAHII)

Eligibility Criteria

Membership is open to anyone working in public health or the wider health sector, and those studying for a health-related degree in colleges and universities across the globe.

  • Fellow: A career commitment to the improvement of health and/or wellbeing, and educated to post-graduate level or equivalent from an academic institution or a recognised professional body, or five years’ experience in a public health related post.
  • Executive: A professional with lifelong achievement in global health advancement.
  • Associate: A career commitment to the improvement of public health and/or wellbeing.
  • Student: Undergraduates of colleges and medical institutions across the globe. Students undertaking a full-time undergraduate or post-graduate qualification in public health or a related health discipline.

Join AHII

Follow the steps below to join:

  1. Sign up for an online account and verify your email address.
  2. Log into My AHII and complete the AHII membership application form.
  3. Choose your membership level and payment type (you can pay via invoice, credit card, or direct debit).
  4. Submit your form; our membership team will review your application.
  5. Your membership is confirmed and we will send you a membership certificate.

Membership Fees

Our standard annual membership fees are:

  • Fellows — $200
  • Executive — $150
  • Associates — $120
  • Students — $50
